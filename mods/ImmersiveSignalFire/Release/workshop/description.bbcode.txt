[h1]Immersive Signal Fire[/h1]

Call nearby tribal and medieval allies with a real smoke signal instead of a radio.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*]Research Smoke signals at Neolithic technology.
[*]Build a cold 2×2 fresh-and-seasoned wood signal stack.
[*]Right-click it with or without a selected colonist.
[*]Choose an allied sub-industrial settlement within ten world tiles.
[*]Assign one caller and up to two helpers in the familiar ritual-style setup window.
[*]Watch the group send a dark compressed SOS pattern for ten seconds.
[/list]

Melee and Social skill determine whether warriors answer immediately, answer later, misunderstand the signal, or never notice it. Every terminal active attempt consumes the single-use wood stack and leaves a substantial patch of cleanable ash.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Harmony
[*]Dynamic Effects Forge
[/list]

Show Me Your Tools or its maintained fork is optional. When present, participants lift a shared smoke blanket in sync with the dark smoke pulses.

This mod does not perform live AI generation. Its original building, smoke, and blanket textures are packaged with the mod.
