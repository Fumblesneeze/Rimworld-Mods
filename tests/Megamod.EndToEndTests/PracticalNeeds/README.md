## Preconditions and action provenance

A/B/C create only owned adult pawns, distinct native policies and two physical Beer per pawn during
paused setup. Traits, health and generated memories are cleared; food/rest/joy are filled; current
mood is set once to 0.40. The actual Core `NewColonyOptimism` memory is gained once. Its inspected
facts are pinned: one stage, +10 mood, eight days; Core Mood base is 0.32 and MaxLevel is 1. The
actual thought list, stages, offsets and computed initial potential are retained. Initial potential
must be at least 0.60; B's caravan control must also be strictly below 0.90. A bad setup fails rather
than adjusting the potential cache or weakening the assertion. No mood/need/history/inventory
refresh occurs after ordinary native time starts.

Scheduled Beer is enabled, joy/addiction use is disabled, native frequency is one day, joy threshold
is 1, and unrelated policy rows are off. Map stock target is zero; caravan stock target stays two.
Tolerance choices are made through the actual native manager slider. The seven native row fields,
row/policy/drug identities and every owned choice encoding remain pinned around later actions.

native-eligible scheduled attempts. Turning only tolerance off permits B's native dose while A
remains mood-blocked. Restoring tolerance and turning mood off lets A select its native dose. Mood
is turned back on while that same job/source is visibly executing; it finishes once. B's later
rejections prove its native interval, not tolerance independence after consumption.

0.90/Any. Before B's first native dose the exact completed scheduled scope must have S024 on and

The native partial-ingestion split remains recorded for provenance and owned cleanup, but is not
