## Why

Healthy trees should remain standing until harvesting or a meaningful environmental/damage cause
removes them. S077 Trees Don't Just Die supplies the reference for removing their age limit while
preserving growing, forestry and survival rules.

## What Changes

- Specify no age-driven death for the native tree family, including bonsai when present and the
  source's tree-like plants such as saguaro and timbershroom.
- Extend source coverage to compatible nested tree descendants while keeping unrelated crops,
  fibercorn and dead stumps outside this behavior.
- Preserve other causes of damage/death, growth, harvesting, research and native forest population
  rules. Existing plants retain their age, growth and HP; the change neither heals nor resurrects.
- Keep native information/save behavior and guarded, merge-preserving XML changes with explicit
  original-provider authority and startup fallback.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `megamod-tree-longevity`: Trees without age-driven death, with other threats and forestry unchanged.

### Modified Capabilities

None.
