# Core-derived atlas and adjacent-building implementation plan

This plan is normative for the implementation slice that follows the cumulative rendering matrix. It does not authorize generated art or packaged Core pixels.

## Runtime source and atlas model

1. The only structural source families are the installed RimWorld Core `Wall_Atlas_Bricks`, `Wall_Atlas_Planks`, and `Wall_Atlas_Smooth` diffuse/Stuff-mask pairs whose dimensions and accepted hashes are already pinned by the renderer. An unsupported atlas fails closed to its native print.
2. A Thin-only vertex is compiled in a 60 by 60 south-west-origin raster. Its structural key is `(N,E,S,W typed state, doubled-ray mask, door-ray mask, cleared ordinary quadrants)`. Material resolution adds `(source texture identity, source mask identity, shader, Stuff colors, material family, damage grade, door/leaf treatment, partition owner)` without changing topology.
3. The four typed ray states are `None`, `Thin Wall`, and `ordinary wall`, producing all 81 topology states. A Thin-only material may be shared only when the complete structural and material key is equal. No cached plane, post, stitch, cap, or connector is composed over another structural plane.
4. An affected regular wall is compiled once in a 120 by 120 raster. Its unchanged 60 by 60 native tile occupies coordinates 30 through 89 on both axes. The remaining 30-pixel gutter on each side holds only the constant-width Thin continuation, square shoulder contour, and required antialias neighborhood. Runtime submission remains five disjoint sampling partitions: one clamped 60 by 60 native tile and the four clamped exterior rings. Their visible alpha may meet at a boundary but may not overlap.
5. A hybrid cache key includes the concrete regular material and linked slot, all incident exact vertex/ray descriptors, the Thin source material/Stuff/family/damage/door identity for every contact, doubled ownership, side-T ownership, and the boundary-balance recipe version. Removing or changing any participant invalidates every affected vertex, regular tile, shadow plan, and adjacent-building visual-offset cache entry.
6. The complete non-shadow straight horizontal structural envelope is rows 5 through 39 before balance, including the two-pixel contour and one-pixel antialias ring. Its source-pixel edge extents are therefore `[5,40]`, whose midpoint is 22.5. The linked raster compiler translates only horizontal structural/contour samples by exactly eight rows, producing final edge extents `[13,48]` around shared-boundary coordinate 30: 17 pixels on one side and 18 on the other. The one-pixel difference is the closest integral 60-pixel raster representation of the ideal 7.5-pixel correction. The complete north-south envelope is columns 13 through 46, with edge extents `[13,47]` and midpoint 30, so it is not translated on X. Every render plane and endpoint remains centered on its canonical grid edge/vertex; the balance is part of the one linked raster rather than a world-space shift that could move north-south endpoints longitudinally.
7. Hybrid native and exterior samples remain phase-locked to their 120 by 120 world raster. Horizontal Thin gutter/aperture samples receive the same eight-row translation inside that single composition before the five disjoint crops are emitted; north-south samples remain at their exact vertex rows. The native wall is never overpainted by a shifted connector plane.
8. Sun shadows do not use the structural Z correction. Their physical base is exactly centered on the canonical edge and uses `1 by 7/60` or `7/60 by 1` before RimWorld applies its celestial cast vector.

## Adjacent-building visual displacement

1. Building placement and construction remain entirely footprint based. For every rotation, every internal cardinal adjacency of the final occupied `CellRect` is tested against completed Thin structures plus Thin blueprints and frames. Any matching shared edge rejects the building. The inverse wall-on-existing-footprint path applies the same geometric predicate.
2. A completed ordinary building, its blueprint, and its frame receive a render-only offset when a Thin Wall or Thin Door occupies an exterior perimeter edge of that footprint. Walls, ordinary doors, Thin structures, pawns, items, floors, zones, interaction cells, reservations, pathing, rooms, and collision are excluded.
3. The displacement for the pinned opposing Core Hand Tailoring Bench comparator is direction-specific, not one shared quantum. Its north/south completed and blueprint source canvas remains 224 by 96 with nonzero-alpha bound `[16,16,208,89)`, but the renderer measures the actual current Thing/style graphic, horizontal plane-size swap or rotated plane, UV flip, per-rotation `Graphic.DrawOffset`, phase, and final wall-facing structural band. A Core Frame is a distinct phase: its actual always-present `1.15x` footprint underfield/corner envelope is measured instead of reusing the completed bench sprite, producing `23/60` north/south clearance for the pinned 3x1 frame. An absent, custom-UV, transparent, or otherwise unmeasurable graphic fails closed at its native draw position. The result rounds each minimum outward displacement upward to the next `1/60` source pixel and retains exactly one source pixel of safety. The superseded `33/60` calculation treated transparent canvas margin as solid; the later superseded symmetric `22/60` calculation split total alpha depth evenly and visibly hid part of the north bench. A north perimeter contact contributes a measured southward clearance, south contributes its independently measured northward clearance, and east/west follow the same rule. Contributions are deduplicated by side, signed values combine, and perpendicular sides compose. The final promoted crop must independently prove that both building silhouettes are intact and minimally separated from the wall.
4. The offset changes only the realtime draw center and the matching cached map-mesh graphic/shadow center. `Position`, `Rotation`, occupied cells, interaction cells, selection identity, jobs, bills, reachability, room membership, and save data remain unchanged. A cheap perimeter check runs before graphic/material resolution or any GPU readback, so a map or building with no adjacent Thin edge pays no alpha-measurement cost. Successful measurements are cached by actual graphic/texture/rotation/flip/draw-size/draw-offset/phase plus the owning Thing and dedicated Thin-edge visual revision, so completion, blueprint-to-frame transitions, style changes, destruction, and load cannot retain a stale displacement; unrelated ordinary buildings, doors, terrain, and path-cost events do not invalidate it.

## Acceptance inventory

- Mechanically enumerate every internal edge of representative 1x2, 2x1, 2x3, and 3x2 footprints in all four rotations and prove completed/blueprinted/framed Thin Walls and Thin Doors reject a crossing while all exterior contacts remain legal.
- Mechanically prove the measured Core texture/canvas/alpha bound, all direction/rotation-specific single-side visual offsets, signed combination, perpendicular composition, phase equality, final-pixel non-overlap/minimal clearance, and unchanged logical footprint.
- Mechanically prove straight, endpoint, L, T, +, door-frame, and exposed hybrid-gutter structural bounds satisfy the one-source-pixel balance rule after the shared transform.
- In a fresh minimized Gateway run, use native designators to place furnishings and workbenches on both sides of horizontal and north-south Thin Walls/Doors, capture close/ordinary/far views with cell boundaries, and observe that placement, use, rendering, square regular-wall merges, and centered shadows all remain coherent.
