## ADDED Requirements

Owning mod: **Thin Walls** (`fumblesneeze.thinwalls`) at `mods/ThinWalls`.

### Requirement: Every construction phase uses the same owned-edge topology
The designator preview, blueprint, frame, completed Thin Wall, completed Thin Door, selection bracket, damage, and shadow SHALL resolve the same owned north/east/south/west edge and the same two grid vertices. Phase changes MAY substitute legal state materials or door motion, but SHALL NOT change the edge, footprint, projection, linked endpoints, or regular-wall aperture.

#### Scenario: Four cardinal phases align
- **WHEN** north, east, south, and west Thin Walls and Thin Doors are previewed and constructed on known cells
- **THEN** every phase resolves the same designated edge, uses the same boundary-balanced normal offset, and shares the completed topology silhouette

#### Scenario: Hover and drag previews communicate the authority change
- **WHEN** the player rotates a one-cell hover preview and then extends the mouse drag into a cardinal multi-cell line
- **THEN** the preview first shows the selected edge and changes the complete line to the drag vector's right-hand edge only after a second distinct cell participates

#### Scenario: Free owner cell stays readable
- **WHEN** a pawn and a compatible building occupy a Thin structure's owner cell
- **THEN** the edge structure remains on the perimeter and no full-cell wall plane, shadow, damage mark, or door art covers the usable cell center

#### Scenario: Furnishings on both sides remain visually intact
- **WHEN** workbenches or other compatible buildings occupy both cells adjacent to a Thin Wall or Thin Door without crossing its edge
- **THEN** the edge structure remains aligned to their shared perimeter and each eligible completed/blueprint/frame sprite is shifted independently by its minimum direction-specific measured nonzero-alpha clearance, without clipping, erasure, a full-cell plane, wall overdraw through the building art, visual fusion, or a conspicuous artificial aisle
- **AND** both logical footprints, interaction cells, jobs, reservations, pathing, room membership, and save data remain unchanged

### Requirement: Adjacent ordinary buildings receive only a bounded render offset
For an eligible ordinary building, blueprint, or frame, the renderer SHALL inspect only Thin Wall and Thin Door structures on the exterior perimeter of its final occupied `CellRect`. It SHALL perform that cheap perimeter test before resolving a graphic/material or reading texture pixels. For each contacted side it SHALL resolve the actual current Thing/style graphic and its actual native draw path. Cached `Graphic.Print` keeps rotated flip UVs unmirrored and uses the `180` degree angle from `Graphic.AngleFromRot`, while realtime `Graphic.DrawWorker` uses its flipped mesh UVs as well as that angle. Non-rotated cached printing uses its UV flip and `GraphicData.flipExtraRotation`, while realtime drawing uses the flipped mesh without that cached-print angle. The corresponding plane-size swap or mesh rotation, per-rotation draw offset, Stuff/material, draw size, and nonzero-alpha silhouette for that phase are authoritative. A Core Frame SHALL use its actual always-present `1.15x` footprint underfield/corner envelope rather than the completed building sprite; an attachment Frame SHALL additionally include vanilla's `Rotation.AsVector2 * 0.5` draw-center translation. Projecting that silhouette and the final outlined Thin structural silhouette onto the fixed-camera normal axis, it SHALL choose the smallest outward displacement, rounded upward to one `1/60`-cell source-pixel quantum, that leaves at least one source pixel between the building's wall-facing opaque/antialiased pixels and the Thin structure's opaque/antialiased pixels. Transparent canvas margin SHALL NOT be treated as opaque geometry. North, east, south, and west contacts SHALL be measured independently; opposite rotations or opposite sides SHALL NOT be assumed symmetric. Multiple segments on one side count once, opposite signed contributions cancel only when their independently measured magnitudes are equal, and perpendicular sides compose. An absent, transparent, custom-UV, or otherwise unmeasurable phase graphic SHALL fail closed at the native draw position without throwing.

The pinned Core Hand Tailoring Bench comparator SHALL retain its `224x96` source canvas and `[16,16,208,89)` nonzero-alpha source bound as a regression input, but its north-facing and south-facing clearances SHALL be derived from the wall-facing directional half of the final sprite rather than from half of the total 73-row alpha depth. The earlier symmetric `22/60` rule is superseded because the accepted in-game crop proves it hides part of the north workbench behind the wall. A promoted two-sided capture SHALL additionally measure the final screen-space structural and workbench alpha components and fail if any workbench opaque/antialias pixel is occluded by, fused with, or lies behind the wall component, or if the nearest separation exceeds the one-pixel safety band by more than one `1/60` quantum.

The offset SHALL modify only the realtime draw center and the corresponding cached map-mesh graphic/shadow center. It SHALL exclude regular walls, ordinary doors, Thin structures, pawns, items, floors, zones, and any Thing that is not a completed building, building blueprint, or building frame. Its measurement cache SHALL include the actual graphic/texture, rotation, native print-versus-realtime path, flip, flip-induced angle, draw size, draw offset, attachment-frame offset, and phase; its per-Thing cache identity SHALL additionally include the owning Thing and a dedicated Thin-edge visual revision so style or construction phase changes, edge addition/removal, and load cannot leave a stale displacement, while unrelated ordinary buildings, doors, terrain, and path-cost changes do not invalidate it.

#### Scenario: Two buildings move away from one shared Thin edge
- **WHEN** eligible buildings occupy the two cells adjacent to one horizontal or north-south Thin Wall or Thin Door
- **THEN** their rendered centers move by the independently measured minimum components in opposite directions away from the edge while their logical positions and interaction cells remain unchanged, neither sprite is bisected by the complete rendered wall body, and no larger empty aisle is introduced

#### Scenario: Opposite contacts cancel and corner contacts compose
- **WHEN** an eligible building has Thin structures on two opposite exterior sides
- **THEN** the independently measured signed contributions are combined and cancel only when equal
- **WHEN** it instead has contacts on two perpendicular sides
- **THEN** both independently measured components are applied as one bounded diagonal without changing footprint legality

### Requirement: The complete structural projection is balanced across the shared cell boundary
The canonical `SharedEdge` SHALL remain the collision, placement, room, selection, and topology identity. Independently, the renderer SHALL position the completed structural projection so that it consumes equal visible space from the two cells adjacent to that edge. For this requirement, the structural projection consists of every final Thin-owned top, front, side, exterior-contour, antialias, closed-door leaf/frame, and clipped damage pixel after topology composition. It excludes directional `SunShadowFade` output, selection brackets, and pixels belonging to a regular wall's native in-cell body.

Let `b` be the fixed-camera projection of the shared cell boundary onto the normal axis of a straight Thin segment, and let `min` and `max` be the final structural-alpha bounds on that axis at the 60-source-pixel-per-cell contract scale. The renderer SHALL satisfy `abs((b - min) - (max - b)) <= 1` source pixel. A horizontal Thin segment SHALL therefore divide its complete visible wall body equally between the cells north and south of the edge; a north-south Thin segment SHALL divide it equally between the cells west and east of the edge. The balance point is the midpoint of the complete structural silhouette, not necessarily the midpoint of the seven-pixel dark top band.

The normal offset derived from that invariant SHALL be identical for designator ghost, blueprint, frame, completed Thin Wall, closed Thin Door, fixed frame, selection target, and damage. Door motion MAY translate leaves only along the edge and SHALL NOT change their normal offset. Every exposed constant-width Thin arm outside an ordinary wall tile SHALL retain the same boundary-balanced cross-section inside straight, L, T, +, endpoint, and regular-wall-contact states. A junction MAY occlude an internal face, but it SHALL NOT displace the surviving Thin arm into a cell center or assign the entire projection to one adjacent cell.

#### Scenario: Horizontal structural body is boundary-balanced
- **WHEN** a horizontal Thin Wall is rendered over a visible north/south cell boundary
- **THEN** the complete non-shadow structural alpha extends equally into the two adjacent cells within one source pixel and no phase moves the body toward either cell center

#### Scenario: North-south structural body is boundary-balanced
- **WHEN** a north-south Thin Wall is rendered over a visible west/east cell boundary
- **THEN** the complete non-shadow structural alpha extends equally into the two adjacent cells within one source pixel and is not centered inside either cell

#### Scenario: Two-sided furnishings retain maximum visible area
- **WHEN** compatible buildings or workbenches are placed in both adjacent cells through the native player workflow
- **THEN** each building retains the same maximum unobscured interior-side area because the Thin structure contributes no greater normal intrusion to one side than to the other

#### Scenario: Junctions preserve the straight-arm balance
- **WHEN** an exposed Thin arm participates in any Thin-only or mixed ordinary/thin L, T, +, endpoint, or side-T state
- **THEN** the arm's normal structural bounds remain the same boundary-balanced bounds as the corresponding straight arm outside the genuinely occluded intersection

### Requirement: Structural art is deterministic and derived from installed Core materials
Thin Walls SHALL NOT use an image-generation model or generated image as source, candidate, intermediate, packaged texture, preview, title, or publishing input. Structural rendering SHALL sample installed RimWorld Core wall and door materials at runtime through a deterministic, versioned topology compiler. Core pixels SHALL NOT be copied into the mod package. Stony Thin structures SHALL use the Bricks wall appearance, Woody structures SHALL use Planks as their base, and Metallic structures SHALL use Smooth as their base. Any OSB flake/panel marks, plate seams, rivets, door details, damage marks, icons, masks, or state treatments SHALL be produced only by checked-in deterministic code or numeric/vector recipes.

The exact runtime atlas/cache partition, key fields, 60/120-pixel coordinate spaces, boundary-balance transform, invalidation, and acceptance inventory SHALL follow `references/2026-08-19-core-derived-atlas-and-adjacent-building-plan.md`. The implementation SHALL use transient runtime textures only; it SHALL NOT add a shipped combinatorial atlas or package any derived Core pixels.

Image-generated out-of-game concept sheets MAY be used only as ignored, disposable requirements-elicitation aids. They SHALL NOT become normative visual references or implementation inputs. Before another concept-sheet iteration is reviewed, every accepted or rejected observation from the prior iteration SHALL be copied into the normative rendering requirement matrix, and the next sheet SHALL be audited cumulatively against every active row rather than only the latest prompt.

#### Scenario: Clean package contains no generated wall art
- **WHEN** the Release package and its source manifest are inspected
- **THEN** no rejected wall/post/stitch/connector/jamb bitmap, image-generation prompt/output/provenance, or copied Core wall pixel asset is present
- **THEN** every shipped raster has a declared non-generative recipe or is a reviewed in-game publishing capture

#### Scenario: Stuff appearances remain distinct
- **WHEN** otherwise identical granite, wood, and steel Thin Walls are viewed at the same zoom
- **THEN** they inherit Bricks, Planks plus deterministic OSB face marks, and Smooth plus deterministic plate/rivet face marks respectively while retaining one identical physical silhouette and ordinary Stuff tinting

### Requirement: The renderer compiles one linked union per grid vertex
At each integer grid vertex, the renderer SHALL classify north, east, south, and west incident edge rays as `None`, `Thin`, or `Ordinary`, retaining the north-east/north-west/south-east/south-west regular-wall quadrant mask, Thin Wall versus Thin Door identity, damage owner, and phase. Each occupied ordinary quadrant SHALL reference the matching quadrant of the concrete resolved Core tile, including its real alpha and surface classes; it SHALL NOT be treated as a solid floor square. `Ordinary` SHALL dominate where ordinary and thin surface ownership actually overlaps. The topology compiler SHALL accept all `3^4 = 81` directional arm combinations and every ordinary-quadrant mask, and SHALL return one deterministic sprite-space union containing every requested edge ray and occupied atlas quadrant. One stable render owner SHALL emit the complete local plan. The renderer SHALL NOT emit independently outlined arm sprites at that vertex.

The final union SHALL have exactly one exterior contour and no internal endpoint contour. The compiler SHALL finish the analytic top footprint union before it derives any front face, lateral side, exterior outline, antialias, or shadow casting edge. Only a boundary of that final union may create one of those features. A collinear or perpendicular continuation therefore removes the participating ray's terminal face/cap automatically, and a horizontal endpoint's top, front, and contour terminate at the same requested grid vertex without a longitudinal side slab or overhang. Straight, exposed-end, L, T, and + Thin-only shapes SHALL arise from the same constant-width linked topology rule. The genuinely exposed south facade of a north-going arm remains the explicit fixed-camera full-height endpoint treatment. Once either horizontal wall or fixed door frame participates at that vertex, the north-going arm is no longer an exposed endpoint and its complete 28-by-22 terminal facade SHALL be suppressed before the connected horizontal wall facade or three-pixel frame is applied. Junction posts, endpoint caps, crowns, pillars, collinear stitches, chosen bridge rays, folded multi-ray stars, geometry miters, exterior wedges, chamfers, independently printed ramps, and coplanar per-arm connector decals are forbidden. A one-pixel Core-derived internal material-phase seam is not geometry and is required below at a perpendicular surface union. Every mixed-width change SHALL keep a square orthogonal exterior shoulder at the regular tile perimeter. No incident arm may be suppressed merely to simplify the draw order.

At a perpendicular Thin-only L, T, or + union, the horizontal top/facade SHALL remain the fixed-camera foreground surface and continue through the complete crossing. The incident north-south top surface SHALL terminate at the crossing through one-pixel dark Core-derived diagonal material-phase seams connecting the analytic corners of the two top rectangles. The seams SHALL change only the color/material sample of already-structural pixels: they SHALL NOT change alpha, width, exterior contour, shadow mask, collision, or ownership, and SHALL NOT leave a diagonal gap or sloped exterior silhouette. An L has the one seam required by its occupied quadrant, a T has the corresponding pair, and a + has the symmetric pair on each visible north-south approach. Equivalent rotations/reflections SHALL preserve this ownership grammar under the fixed RimWorld camera.

At an exact Thin-to-regular-wall contact, the mixed-width silhouette and outer shoulder remain square while every pre-existing structural byte of the receiving regular-wall top remains unchanged. The Thin-only diagonal material-phase grammar does not propagate into a regular wall tile. A one-tile end contact and both halves of a two-tile side-T SHALL therefore contain no added diagonal, V mark, miter, atlas-turn highlight, brace, or dark phase stroke. Thin Doors retain the same seam-free receiving-wall rule through only their fixed-frame contact.

The boundary-balancing translation SHALL NOT disconnect the structural planes at a Thin-only vertex. When a south-going north-south wall meets at least one non-door horizontal wall, its seven-pixel structure SHALL continue from the south half through the translated horizontal top band before foreground occlusion is applied. The complete seven-pixel bridge at final raster rows `31..37` SHALL remain structural but SHALL be visibly classified and sampled as the incident non-door horizontal front face; only rows `38..44` SHALL remain visible as top behind it. When every incident horizontal arm is a Thin Door, there is no horizontal facade to own that bridge: only each door's three-pixel fixed-frame interval SHALL continue through rows `31..37` as `Top`, and the rest of the seven- or fourteen-pixel South width SHALL stay transparent so no moving-leaf aperture is filled. If one horizontal wall and one horizontal door meet the same South wall, the non-door wall's complete facade bridge wins.

A north-going wall has a full `28x22` south-facing facade only while its vertex is an exposed endpoint. At any north-plus-horizontal L/T vertex, the complete North-owned terminal projection across its derived side envelope and rows `8..37`—the facade at `8..29` plus every top/side continuation below the horizontal top—SHALL be removed first. Each incident non-door horizontal half then owns only its corresponding horizontal front rows `16..37`; an incident horizontal Thin Door restores only its three fixed-frame columns as horizontal front rows `16..37`. The continuing North member SHALL meet the horizontal top beginning at row `38`. This clipping respects the seven- or fourteen-pixel North width and composes deterministically when the opposite horizontal arm is a wall or another door; a non-door wall owns the shared center column ahead of a door. The final structural union SHALL contain no transparent one-to-three-pixel notch between requested fixed structural planes, no rectangular top tab hanging below a horizontal face, no wall slab inside a door aperture, no opposite-half terminal projection, and no internal outline around a bridge. The north-south member SHALL read behind a horizontal wall or connect only to a door's fixed frame rather than as a post pasted onto its face.

### Requirement: Core top, face, side, and outline dimensions are preserved independently
The renderer SHALL use the pinned RimWorld 1.6.4871 Core 320×320 four-by-four wall atlas layout and SHALL verify the installed source identity before accepting it. Each 80×80 slot SHALL be sampled through the same inner 60×60 region as `MaterialAtlasPool`: ten source pixels of gutter per side, scale `0.1875`, and the vanilla link-index order `None=0, North=1, East=2, South=4, West=8`.

For the horizontal `East|West` source, the compiler SHALL treat rows 0–1 as the north outline, rows 2–34 as the 33-row top plane, rows 35–56 as the 22-row front face, and rows 57–59 as the antialiased/south outline. For the vertical `North|South` source, it SHALL treat columns 0–2 as the west outline, 3–13 as the eleven-column west side, 14–46 as the 33-column top plane, 47–56 as the ten-column east side, and 57–59 as the east outline. Bricks, Planks, and Smooth SHALL share these geometry landmarks.

A Thin arm SHALL narrow only the 33/60-cell top footprint to `7/60` cell. It SHALL preserve the complete 22/60-cell front height and the complete 11/60 plus 10/60-cell north-south side projection. At an exposed south-facing end of a north-south arm, the front SHALL span the complete side-projected envelope (`16..43` for a seven-column top) for all 22 facade rows while the top remains only `27..33`; rendering that endpoint as a seven-column needle or outline tail is forbidden. These are sprite-space bands in the fixed RimWorld camera projection: horizontal and north-south forms SHALL be compiled independently from their matching Core bands, never produced by rotating one result or by treating the visible facade as a physical ground-plane thickness. The compiler SHALL map the appropriate Core top/front/side UV bands onto the resulting polygons and draw the exterior contour around the final union. It SHALL NOT scale, rotate, flatten, or crop the complete wall sprite to obtain thinness.

In the final boundary-balanced 60×60 south-west-origin runtime tile, a horizontal straight SHALL occupy top rows `38..44` and the complete front rows `16..37`. A north-south straight SHALL occupy top columns `27..33`, west-side columns `16..26`, and east-side columns `34..43`. A north-only endpoint SHALL additionally occupy front rows `8..29` across columns `16..43`. The final union SHALL then receive exactly two solid-dark exterior pixels plus one antialias ring. These coordinates SHALL be asserted independently for horizontal, north-south, and exposed endpoint output.

The Core Bricks top/front luminance ratio (`107.92/228.88`, approximately `0.472`) and top/mean-side ratio (`109.41/173.09`, approximately `0.632`) SHALL remain within ±0.05 after material tinting in the neutral-source contract. The exterior contour SHALL retain Core's two solid-dark source-equivalent pixels plus its antialias transition. Top surfaces SHALL therefore remain significantly darker than lit faces at ordinary and far useful zoom. A one-cell north-south sample SHALL retain its complete one-cell longitudinal extent, both asymmetric side bands, and its complete south-end facade when exposed; mip generation and filtering SHALL NOT collapse that sample into a square post, floor rail, or unrelated furniture silhouette.

#### Scenario: Horizontal wall stays full height
- **WHEN** a horizontal Thin Wall is compared with a same-material Core wall at the same zoom
- **THEN** its front face preserves the Core wall's three-course 22/60-cell height while only its top footprint is narrowed to 7/60 cell

- **THEN** its eleven-column and ten-column side projections remain visible around the seven-column-equivalent top, its exposed south end shows a complete 28-column three-course facade instead of a needle tail, and no horizontal facade or junction surface is pasted over it

#### Scenario: Far-zoom readability
- **WHEN** wood, stone, and metal topology states are viewed at far useful zoom without selection brackets
- **THEN** the dark top/light face separation, exterior outline, and orientation remain discernible

### Requirement: Thin runs have continuous material detail and no cell-boundary artifacts
Surface detail SHALL be mapped in world-space phase across repeated linked arms. Horizontal Thin top/facade pixels SHALL always use the Core `East|West` straight donor. North-south Thin top and side coverage, alpha, Stuff mask, and directional profile SHALL come from the Core `North|South` straight donor; for Stone only, each visible west/east side color sample exclusively causally owned by North/South arms SHALL borrow the phase-aligned three-course detail from the Core `East|West` facade row matching its side depth while retaining that link-5 alpha and mask. Horizontal endpoint side caps and H/V shared-owner intersection caps inside a mixed junction SHALL retain their native donor and SHALL NOT enter this north-south-only remap. The south-visible endpoint facade of a north-south arm SHALL use the same three-course `East|West` facade donor. This mapping SHALL remain unchanged inside L/T/+ unions. A non-straight Core atlas slot MAY donate its native ordinary-wall quadrant but SHALL NOT donate interior fill, bevel, turn-highlight, or endpoint pixels to a Thin arm; no pale smear or topology-slot artifact may interrupt a continuous material run. Stone SHALL use Core Bricks' three-course running bond; every mortar joint SHALL stop at a course boundary and no tile/owner boundary SHALL create a full-height line through two or three courses. Wood OSB and metal plate/rivet marks SHALL be clipped to the visible face polygons before union and SHALL NOT cross the dark top, exterior contour, door opening, or regular-wall material boundary. Repeated cells SHALL share one deterministic phase so a straight run reads as one wall.

Wood and metal panel divisions SHALL be staggered within their face courses and offset from the 30-pixel vertex partition and 60-pixel cell boundaries. No material-detail operation may draw a full-height dark line at either boundary; repeated endpoint/partition seams that disclose sprite tiling are forbidden.

#### Scenario: Six-cell stone runs remain continuous

### Requirement: Regular walls and Thin structures form one hybrid linked topology
When a completed Thin Wall or Thin Door reaches the exact perimeter or endpoint of a completed Core or compatible standard linked `building.isWall` tile, the regular wall SHALL use its ordinary linked atlas state with the touching direction included. Its ordinary-width body runs normally to the tile perimeter; the Thin structure begins outside that perimeter at constant width. The width change is therefore one abrupt square butt joint at the centered native-tile boundary, not a transition distributed through the regular cell. Offset or parallel silhouettes that do not share the same grid vertex/perimeter SHALL remain independent and SHALL NOT infer a connection from proximity or screen-space overlap.

The renderer SHALL suppress each affected wall's native atlas print and compile one `120x120` hybrid runtime raster for that wall from the same resolved diffuse, Stuff mask, colors, and link-state sources. The exact native `60x60` tile SHALL occupy pixels `30..89` on both axes at unchanged texel density. For filtered sampling only, that one raster SHALL be partitioned into a tightly cropped/clamped `60x60` native texture on a `1x1` quad and four independently cropped/clamped exterior textures on four quads that tile the `2x2` ring outside that native cell. Each exterior crop SHALL contain only the pixels submitted by its matching south, north, west, or east ring quad; its inner texture boundary SHALL be the actual gutter boundary, so every mip level terminates in submitted contact pixels rather than averaging transparent pixels from the unsubmitted native hole. No exterior guard pixel or transparent native-hole pixel may be included in an exterior crop. The five visible structural partitions SHALL never overlap. Every mip level of both native and exterior contact textures therefore remains opaque at a structural shared boundary, preventing the join between two receiving regular tiles from becoming an alpha edge. A simple end/corner contact affects one regular wall tile and may add one square ordinary-width arm inside that tile before narrowing at its perimeter. A side-T contact at the shared vertex of two collinear regular wall tiles affects both tiles differently: the native straight run remains byte-identical except for the two halves of the seven-pixel contact contour band, where the original outline/antialias bytes are replaced by phase-aligned regular-material structure from the incident Thin top/front/side cross-section; exactly one canonical padded tile owns the exterior constant-width Thin gutter. No ordinary-width perpendicular arm, collar, plate, cap, projected shoulder band, or augmented T-slot bitmap may be painted over the continuous receiving run. Both adjacent tiles SHALL replace only their vertex-facing half of the separating contour so the Thin centerline lands on the shared grid vertex without a transparent slit or regenerated outline. In the gutter, the Thin donor supplies the constant-width half-arm and its complete projected face/side bands. No ordinary-width donor pixel may enter the gutter.

For a south-going Thin endpoint meeting an east-west regular run, the north-west and north-east regular tiles SHALL contribute the west/east halves of only the seven-pixel contour replacement; the deterministic center pixel and exterior gutter remain owned once. A north-going endpoint uses the corresponding south-west/south-east pair. East- and west-going endpoints are exact rotations of the same rule. Stuff-mask, alpha, outline, antialias, damage-boundary, shadow geometry, and diffuse SHALL use that same narrow vertex-centered contact without adding a diagonal internal seam to the receiving regular-wall top. The two regular-wall replacements SHALL meet on the grid vertex without a gap, overlap, doubled contour, pasted rectangle, diagonal exterior geometry, or one-cell lateral displacement.

The compositor SHALL construct analytic original and augmented Core semantic plans before consulting texture bytes. Each plan SHALL label `Top`, `Front`, `WestSide`, `EastSide`, `Outline`, and `OutlineAntialias` pixels and their owning rays from the measured Core geometry: central top `x=14..46,y=25..57`, requested ordinary arms to their tile boundaries, complete `22/11/10` face/side extrusion, and a final two-pixel contour plus one-pixel antialias ring. Actual alpha may validate a semantically owned sample but SHALL NOT decide whether it is an endpoint cap, ordinary body, or outgoing arm. Every analytically required added-arm pixel SHALL resolve to an opaque byte in its mapped straight donor. If that byte is absent or transparent, composition SHALL fail closed before native-print suppression; it SHALL NOT consult the non-straight augmented slot as a fallback. Hybrid compilation SHALL be enabled only for the three pinned RimWorld 1.6 Core wall atlases (`Wall_Atlas_Bricks`, `Wall_Atlas_Planks`, and `Wall_Atlas_Smooth`) with their measured `320x320` identity and accepted RGBA hash. An unknown texture name, a merely dimension-compatible `320x320` atlas, or a changed Core hash SHALL fail closed to the wall's untouched native print.
