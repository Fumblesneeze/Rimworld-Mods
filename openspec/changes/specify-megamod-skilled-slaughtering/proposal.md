## Why

Animal handling should take the worker's competence and the animal's size into account. Preserve
Skilled Slaughtering's duration model while fixing its mismatched handler/animal timers, which can
make slow slaughter attempts fail repeatedly when the animal resumes wandering.

## Owner

Exactly one owner: **Make Rimworld Great Again**, `fumblesneeze.megamod`, `mods/megamod`.
Target **RimWorld 1.7 beta**. Source S117 is a reference, not a co-owner.

## What Changes

- Specify the source-derived Animals, Melee, global-work-speed, Manipulation and Sight contribution
  to slaughter speed, its 10% minimum and the animal-size-adjusted handling duration.
- Keep native slaughter designation/automatic selection, eligibility, approach, progress, death and
  consequences. Apply the timing consistently to native animal-slaughter jobs, including those
  generated by the slaughterer mental state; do not expand their target choices.
- Coordinate the animal's temporary wait with the same handling duration; preserve interruption,
  release, save/reload and once-only completion. Use a minimum positive duration rather than an
  accidental instant/invalid result at exceptional speed.
- Keep speed information in the existing pawn stats surface, preserve the source's usable stat
  identity where safe, admit compatible additions, and suppress overlapping source implementation.
- Use direct shared composition, guarded additive definitions and minimal per-patch target checks;
  replace the source's retained global pawn cache with current job-local facts.

This is a **specification-only draft**. No gameplay, migration or compatibility is delivered or
accepted. The user's Cooking contribution and slaughter-origin yield change are the separate
[S118 contract](../specify-megamod-slaughter-yield/proposal.md); they compose with this timing
directly and preserve native base yields.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `megamod-skilled-slaughtering`: skill/size-dependent handling, coordinated waits, native outcomes,
  contextual stat information, lifecycle and guarded provider selection.

### Modified Capabilities

None. S008 hunting/finishing and S065 companion finishing remain separate; S118 specifies the
additional Cooking/yield behavior without patches against owned timing code.
